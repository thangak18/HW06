## Anh huong

Cot `password` luu nguyen van. `SELECT *` roi `res.json(user)` dua ca `password` lan `reset_token` ra ngoai. Bat ky ai xem duoc mot response login (log, proxy, cache trinh duyet) deu co mat khau that. Vi nguoi dung thuong dung lai mat khau, thiet hai vuot ra ngoai he thong nay.

## Buoc tai hien

```bash
curl -i -X GET 'http://localhost:3000/api/users/me' \
     -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
     -H 'X-Student-Id: 23127060' \
     -H 'Authorization: Bearer <token>'
```
