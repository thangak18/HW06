## Anh huong

Code tinh `discount = Math.floor(total * (1 - discount_value))`. Voi `discount_value = 10` (nghia la 10%), cong thuc thanh `total * (1 - 10) = total * (-9)`. Voi don 500.000d, `discount_amount` la **-4.500.000** va `final_amount` thanh **5.000.000** - khach hang bi tinh gap muoi lan khi ap ma giam gia. SRS FR-09 ghi ro cong thuc dung la `total * discount_value / 100`.
