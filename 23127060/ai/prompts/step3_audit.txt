and correct the invalid or incomplete ones. You are fully responsible for the final test cases."

Yeu cau:
1. Doi chieu TUNG case trong testcases/API-*_generated.csv voi eshop-sut/README.md (SRS).
   Voi moi case phai tra loi duoc: ky vong nay truy nguoc ve CAU NAO trong dac ta?
   Neu khong truy nguoc duoc thi ky vong do la suy dien, phai ghi nhan.
2. Khong sua tay tung dong. Viet luat ra giay truoc, moi luat bam vao mot cau cu the trong SRS,
   roi ap dung nhat quan cho ca 225 case. Phan doi hoi phan doan rieng (gan lai ma SEC, viet lai
   case sai) de trong bang tra co ly do bang van xuoi cho tung dong.
3. Sua ngay cac case INVALID va INCOMPLETE trong file *_audited.csv, ghi ro da sua gi vao cot
   Audit_Note.
4. KHONG duoc dieu chinh ty le VALID cho dep. Neu ty le lech nhieu so voi du kien thi phai
   giai thich nguyen nhan, khong duoc ha tieu chuan gan nhan.
5. Kiem tra lai do phu SEC sau khi gan lai ma, va sua luon references/TESTCASE_TAXONOMY.md
   neu chinh taxonomy do minh viet la nguon gay loi.
