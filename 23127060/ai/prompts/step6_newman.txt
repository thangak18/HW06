1. Chay ca 3 collection bang Newman tren localhost, sinh bao cao HTML + JSON.
2. Truoc moi lan chay phai dua CSDL ve trang thai goc. Khong duoc chay hai lan lien tiep tren
   cung mot CSDL: SUT co bug khoa tai khoan nen lan hai se cho ra hang loat that bai gia.
3. Doc ky tung that bai. Phan biet ba loai: (a) that bai co chu dich vi phoi bay bug SUT,
   (b) that bai vi ky vong cua test case sai, (c) that bai vi bo test tu lam nhieu chinh no
   (thu tu chay, du lieu dung chung). Loai (b) va (c) PHAI sua, khong duoc de lan vao so lieu.
4. Bo @contract dung cho CI phai duoc suy ra tu KET QUA CHAY THAT, khong duoc suy ra tu tag
   gan luc thiet ke, va phai ghi ro trong bao cao rang no la moc hoi quy chu khong phai
   "danh sach case dung".
5. Kiem chung header X-Student-Id bang cach doc request.header ma Newman ghi lai, khong chi
   dua vao dong console.log.
6. Moi con so trong bao cao phai sinh tu file newman/*.json, khong duoc go tay.
