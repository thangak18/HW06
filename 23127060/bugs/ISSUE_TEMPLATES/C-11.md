## Anh huong

Payload `<script>` va `<img src=x onerror=>` duoc luu nguyen van vao CSDL va tra ve nguyen van. SEC-04 doi du lieu nguoi dung nhap phai duoc escape khi hien thi. **Gioi han cua phep kiem nay:** o tang API chi chung minh duoc **nua nguon** - rang may chu luu payload tho. Viec no co thuc su chay tren trinh duyet hay khong con phu thuoc vao lop hien thi, phai kiem rieng o tang giao dien.

## Buoc tai hien

```bash
curl -i -X GET 'http://localhost:3000/api/products/19' \
     -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
     -H 'X-Student-Id: 23127060'
```

## Ket qua thuc te

```http
HTTP/1.1 200
Content-Type: application/json; charset=utf-8

{"id":19,"name":"<script>alert('23127060')</script>","price":1000,"description":"<img src=x onerror=alert(1)>","imageUrl":"","category_id":1}
```

## Ket qua mong doi

Theo `SEC-04` trong dac ta cua he thong.

## De xuat sua

Escape khi hien thi (khong dung `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`), va loc dau vao ngay o tang API nhu mot lop phong thu thu hai.
